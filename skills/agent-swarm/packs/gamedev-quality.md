# Game quality bar (gamedev pack)

Every task in the gamedev pack is judged against this bar. Replace or extend it with your own file (set `quality:` in your pack).

1. **No stand-ins.** Nothing shown as done uses capsules, cubes or grey boxes as characters, creatures or key props. Blockout geometry is fine for level layout only, and must be labelled as blockout.
2. **Motion from the first build.** Anything that moves has real animation: idle, locomotion blended by speed, turns and transitions. Feet plant without sliding, and hands meet what they hold.
3. **Feedback on every action.** Hits, pickups, damage and UI presses each get a sound and a visual. Where it fits, they also get camera or controller feedback.
4. **Readable.** Silhouettes read at a distance. UI is legible at the smallest supported resolution. Important things stand out.
5. **Consistent.** Scale, palette, lighting and style match the art direction across all assets.
6. **Stable.** Normal play gives no console errors, and the frame rate holds its target on the target hardware.
7. **Evidence.** A task counts as done only when it has been shown: screenshots or a short capture for every visual claim, plus the test steps that were run.
