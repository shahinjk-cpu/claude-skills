# PPT Master — Executive Presentation & Slide Design

Designs world-class executive presentation decks, pitch decks, and technical keynotes using clean typographic hierarchy and high-impact layouts.

## Slide Design Principles
1. **One Idea Per Slide**: Never overcrowd; each slide must deliver one clear takeaway.
2. **16:9 Canvas Hierarchy**: Bold headline (44pt+), concise subhead (20pt), visual anchor (chart, diagram, or single metric).
3. **Restrained Color Palette**: One primary dark canvas (`#0A0A0A` or `#1E293B`), one high-contrast accent color (e.g. Electric Blue or Vivid Amber).
