# Cal.com DIY — Self-Hosted Scheduling Engine

Configures and automates Cal.com instances for zero-commission, privacy-first meeting booking and scheduling.

## Features
- Webhook notifications on `BOOKING_CREATED`, `BOOKING_RESCHEDULED`.
- Automated video room creation (Daily.co, Google Meet, Zoom, Jitsi).
- Round-robin and collective team scheduling logic.
