# 🎨 3D & Digital Art Direction Styles Suite

Multi-genre visual aesthetic directions for 3D modeling and rendering: Anime, Chibi, Cartoon, Comic book, Manga, Pixel art, HD-2D, Retro 8-bit/16-bit, Low-poly, Voxel, Realistic, Painterly, Noir, Frutiger Aero, Folk horror, and Minimalist.

This is an **Automated Consolidated Modular Suite**. Rather than cluttering the catalog with dozens of separate micro-skills, this suite centralizes all related domain knowledge while keeping context and token costs minimal.
