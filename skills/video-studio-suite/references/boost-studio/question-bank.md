# Question bank (intake)

Ask at most 4 per message. Always add a recommended answer. Skip anything already known from the request, `brand.json`, `voice.md` or `MOTION.md`.

## Always needed (ask only if unknown)
1. **Brand and networks.** Which brand, and which networks (Instagram, Facebook)? Recommend: the brand named in the request, both networks.
2. **Goal and action.** What should the viewer do after (message us, visit the site, follow, buy)? Recommend one action only.
3. **Audience.** Who exactly is it for (shop owners, shoppers, new followers)? Recommend the brand's main audience from `voice.md`.
4. **Language of captions and on-screen text.** Kurdish Sorani, Arabic, English? Recommend the brand default. Remind: the owner writes or approves Kurdish; Claude's Kurdish is a draft.

## Ask when it matters
5. **Length.** 8, 15 or 30 seconds? Recommend 15.
6. **Proof or offer.** Any real number, price, result, delivery time or warranty to show? If none, say the video will show no numbers.
7. **Material available.** Existing video, photos, logo, screenshots, a sketch? A sketch plus the exact on-screen words gives the best result.
8. **Voice.** No voice (text, sound effects, quiet music) or a Kurdish voiceover? Recommend no voice unless the owner has a script. A voice uses ElevenLabs credits; say so.
9. **Post date and time.** Needed for the schedule, not for production.
10. **Reference.** A reel you like (link) to borrow the structure from? Credit the original creator and do not copy their words.
11. **Things to avoid.** Words, colors, claims, competitors' names.

## How to phrase
- One short message, numbered, each with "Recommended: ...". Example: "1. Action: message us on WhatsApp (recommended). 2. Length: 15 s (recommended)."
- After the answers, repeat the decisions in one line and move to the concept step. Do not ask the same thing twice.
