# 🎬 Master Video Production, Motion & Short-form Suite

Comprehensive video engineering suite covering Remotion, HyperFrames, Sora, YouTube clip generation, FFmpeg 3D scroll animations, Canvas 2D/Web Audio programmatic MP4 generation, and 9:16 short-form video editing with karaoke subtitles and B-roll.

This is an **Automated Consolidated Modular Suite**. Rather than cluttering the catalog with dozens of separate micro-skills, this suite centralizes all related domain knowledge while keeping context and token costs minimal.
