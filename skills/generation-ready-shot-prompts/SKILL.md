# Generation-Ready Shot Prompts

A cinematic taxonomy for converting screenplay scenes into high-yield AI video prompts.

## Shot Structure Formula
`[Subject & Action] + [Camera Motion & Lens] + [Lighting & Atmosphere] + [Environment Details] + [Style/Aesthetic]`

## Example Recipe (Cinematic Thriller)
```text
Medium tracking shot following a woman in a weathered yellow raincoat walking through heavy rain in a neon-lit Tokyo back-alley, 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, reflections of cyan and magenta neon signs in wet asphalt puddles, moody cinematic atmospheric haze, 24fps motion cadence --no blur, no glitch
```
