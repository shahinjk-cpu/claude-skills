# BaseMode — Timeless Minimalist Design Foundations

An unopinionated, razor-sharp CSS and design token foundation focused on editorial typography, fluid spacing, and muted monochrome color palettes.

## Rules of BaseMode
- No superfluous drop shadows or decorative gradients.
- Typography scale calculated with a 1.25 (Major Third) or 1.333 (Perfect Fourth) ratio.
- High typographic contrast: stark black/white text paired with subtle `#737373` neutral borders.
