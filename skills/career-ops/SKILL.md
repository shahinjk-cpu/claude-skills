# Career Ops — Autonomous Job Search & Career Command Center

Manages and accelerates technical career workflows: ATS-tailored resume generation, targeted cover letters, application tracking, and STAR interview preparation.

## Operating Modules
- **Resume Tailoring**: Matches job posting keyword requirements and quantifies bullet points ("Increased X by Y% by implementing Z").
- **STAR Story Bank**: Formats behavioral answers into Situation, Task, Action, Result + Learning.
- **Application Pipeline**: Manages statuses (Wishlist, Applied, Screening, Technical, Offer) in a local ledger.
