# High-Converting SaaS Hero Section Blueprint

A structured blueprint for crafting high-converting hero sections combining technical performance with persuasive UX.

## The 5 Essential Elements
1. **Pill Announcement Badge**: Subtle top pill with release news or changelog link.
2. **H1 Headline with Value Anchor**: Maximum 8–10 words stating exact transformation.
3. **Clarifying Subheadline**: 1–2 sentences explaining how it works and who it's for.
4. **Primary & Secondary Dual CTAs**:
   - Primary: High-contrast button with micro-arrow.
   - Secondary: "View Demo" or "GitHub Repo" link.
5. **Interactive Product Preview**: Live interactive widget, video walkthrough, or 3D canvas preview.
