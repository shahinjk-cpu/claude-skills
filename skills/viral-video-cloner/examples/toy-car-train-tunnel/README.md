# Toy car to real car

A model car rides a sunlit train windowsill, then grows into a real car as the train enters a tunnel. Object swap changes only the car, from a white 1980s sedan to a red sports hatchback. The sill, the forest outside and the tunnel lights are the original pixels.

## Notes

- The constraints pin the timing ("the window goes dark at the same moment") and the framing ("the same size and position in frame"). Without them, the first try entered the tunnel early and the second let the car drive away from the camera.
