# One take, two worlds

A single follow shot of a skateboarder (push, ollie over three cones, grind along a ledge, rock-on finish into the lens) recreated as a Seoul hanok lane in spring and as an anime seaside town. Each world comes from one reference photo and a plan; the people around, the props and the light are redrawn to fit the world while the move and the camera stay.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |

| Output | 16:9 · 10 s · 720p per world |
| Cost | ≈11 credits per world (estimated in October 2026) |

## Try it

Ask your agent, with your own files:

```text
Recreate ./skate.mp4 with me as the skater (me.jpg), on a spring lane in Seoul. Keep every move and the camera.
```

## Plan

[`plan-seoul.json`](plan-seoul.json) and [`plan-anime.json`](plan-anime.json) are what the agent submitted to ChatCut, with project-specific IDs removed and the shot extended to the end of the 10-second range, which the current remake tool requires. Image numbers in a plan refer to the reference images listed above, in that order.

## Source

AI-generated by ChatCut (Gemini Omni), then edited to a plain wide-angle lens. No real person or third-party footage is involved.

## Notes

- The plan maps every prop to the new world: cones become stone lanterns in Seoul and stay cones by the sea; the coffee truck becomes a kei truck.
- New subjects inherit the source's silhouette: the skater wears a beanie in Seoul because the source skater does.
