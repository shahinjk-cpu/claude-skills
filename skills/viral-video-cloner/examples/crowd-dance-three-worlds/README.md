# One crowd take, three worlds

A dense crowd choreography recreated as a white gothic court, a red-feather music-hall revue and a 1930s aquacade, then wiped together on the beat. Every dancer is fictional; the lead and the crowd each come from their own reference image.

## Try it

Ask your agent, with your own files:

```text
Recreate ./dance.mp4 three times: a white gothic court, a red-feather revue and a 1930s aquacade. Lead and crowd from these images.
```

## Plan

The three main-pass plans are included. The final cut also re-rendered two close-ups and added a color grade, so it is not a single render of these plans.

## Source

A music video's choreography, used only as the motion guide. It is not shown or redistributed here.

## Notes

- Headwear silhouettes from the source (hoods) carry into every world: design costumes that have "something on the head" (caps, plumes) and they land naturally.
