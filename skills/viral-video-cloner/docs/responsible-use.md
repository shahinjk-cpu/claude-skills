# Responsible use

Recreating a video can put a real person into footage they never shot. Use it only where
everyone involved would agree.

## People

- Recast only yourself, people who gave you permission, or characters you created.
- Do not recreate public figures: celebrities, athletes, politicians, influencers, or anyone
  whose likeness you have no rights to.
- Do not use a recreation to impersonate someone, fake an endorsement or mislead viewers about
  who said or did something.
- No minors without a guardian's consent, and no sexual or demeaning content involving real people.

## Source videos

- A recreation follows the source's choreography, shots and cut points, and keeps its audio.
  Before you publish, make sure you may use the source that way, or replace the audio.
- Brands, logos and products in the source belong to their owners. Recast them with your own.

## Publishing

- Label AI-generated content where your platform asks for it (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and
  Xiaohongshu all do).
- Follow the terms of the platform you publish on and of the generation models ChatCut uses.

## Reporting

Report misuse or a recreation of you made without consent to team@chatcut.io.
