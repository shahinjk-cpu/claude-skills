# Pricing

- **The skill** is free and open source (Apache-2.0).
- **ChatCut Desktop** is free to download for macOS 13+ and Windows x64.
- **Recreations** are generated in the cloud and need a ChatCut membership. They use the
  membership's credits ([pricing](https://chatcut.io/pricing?utm_source=github&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=oss_viral_cloner)).

## Typical cost

Typical quotes as of November 2026. Your agent always quotes the real estimate before rendering.
Costs listed on the examples were measured in October 2026, so today's quote can be a little higher.

| Length | Resolution | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 s test | 480p | ≈2 |
| 8 s | 720p | ≈10 |
| 10 s | 720p | ≈13 |
| 8 s | 1080p | ≈26 |

## Before every render

Generation starts the moment it is submitted and uses your credits, so your agent tells you the
length and the estimated credits first and waits for your go-ahead.
