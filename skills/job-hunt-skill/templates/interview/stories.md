## Common prompts to prepare
1. Tell me about yourself (2-minute pitch)
2. A project you're proud of / something you built
3. A time a metric or test was misleading
4. A hard call: ship / don't ship, a trade-off under pressure
5. A failure and what you changed
6. Working with stakeholders / remote teams
7. Why this company?
