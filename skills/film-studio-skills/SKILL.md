# Film Studio — Cinematic Production & Direction Suite

Guides agents through end-to-end cinematic storytelling: screenplay format, storyboard pacing, camera cinematography (Dutch angles, rack focus, 180-degree rule), and sound design.

## The Director's Checklist
1. **Visual Language**: Establish color script (cool melancholic teal vs warm nostalgic amber).
2. **Camera Grammar**: Rule of thirds, leading lines, motivated camera movement.
3. **Lighting Rig**: 3-point lighting (Key, Fill, Rim) with volumetric fog and rim separation.
4. **Foley & Soundscape**: Background room tone, proximity-based diegetic sound, and score swells.
